📖 How to Deploy via Remix IDE
1
Open remix.ethereum.org → paste SupplyChain.sol → compile with Solidity 0.8.20
2
In Deploy tab → Environment: Injected Provider - MetaMask → connect your wallet
3
Click Deploy → confirm in MetaMask → copy the deployed contract address
4
Paste that address in the field above and click Save
5
Switch to Sepolia testnet in MetaMask for free test ETH
